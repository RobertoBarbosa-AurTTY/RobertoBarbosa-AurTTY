## 🔬 Research Interests

I'm building toward a research career in **Efficient Deep Learning** — the intersection of mathematics, code and hardware where neural network algorithms are redesigned to do more with less computation, memory and energy.

- **Quantization & low-precision arithmetic** — running networks with fewer bits without losing accuracy
- **Sparsity & matrix approximation** — removing redundant computation from neural networks
- **Numerical methods for ML** — stability and precision of training and inference
- **Algorithm–hardware co-design** — algorithms shaped by how real processors and accelerators work

Compute and energy are today's main bottlenecks for AI. Every mathematical improvement at this level makes models cheaper to train and run for everyone.
